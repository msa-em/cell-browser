title: OSER Assay
numbering:
  enumerator: 21.%s
---

Fluorescent proteins tend to oligomerize when confined to the ER, which can restructure the organelle into organized smooth ER (OSER) whorls [@doi:10.1083/jcb.200306020].
This artifact is sometimes used to assess the propensity of fluorescent proteins to oligomerize [@doi:10.1111/j.1600-0854.2012.01336.x].
Here, six fluorescent proteins were expressed in cells using the CytERM plasmid and the OSER assay was visualized as interactive microscopy maps.
Four fluorescent proteins can be cross-referenced with quantitative scoring [@doi:10.1038/nmeth.3891] that estimated of the percentage of cells expressing mEGFP (98.8±1.2), mCherry (95.0±0.8), FusionRed (91.5±3.0) and mNeonGreen (90.4±2.1) without visible OSER whorls.
