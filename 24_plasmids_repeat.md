These maps correspond to samples from the mCherry-ER plasmid experiment.
Three maps had reduced signal in some areas, perhaps due to an air bubble, so the specimens were reimaged and the original maps were moved here.
Further, the mCherry-Sec61β plasmid transfected with lipofectamine 3000 was imaged twice with different illumination settings.
The repetition with a higher laser intensity (and pixel saturation) was shown here.
